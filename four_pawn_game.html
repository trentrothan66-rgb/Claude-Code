Six Pawn Chess Rules

Setup

  • Played on a standard 8×8 chessboard
  • Only files c through f are used (4 files)
  • White starts with pawns on c2, d2, e2, f2
  • Black starts with pawns on c7, d7, e7, f7

Movement

  • Pawns move exactly as in standard chess
  • Move forward one square to an empty square
  • Move forward two squares from starting rank
  • Capture diagonally forward
  • En passant captures are allowed

Win Conditions

  • Promotion: Advance a pawn to the opponent's back rank (White to rank 8, Black to rank 1) - immediate win
  • Extinction: Capture all opponent's pawns - immediate win
  • Stalemate with advantage: If no legal moves exist and one side has more pawns, that side wins

Draw Condition

  • No legal moves exist and both sides have equal number of pawns
